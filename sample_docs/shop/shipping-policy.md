# Shipping Policy

## Processing time

We roast on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Orders placed before 10am Eastern
ship the same roast day. Other orders ship on the next roast day.

## Rates and free shipping

Standard shipping in the US is a flat $5.95 and takes 3 to 5 business days.
Orders of $40 or more ship free. Express shipping is $14 and takes 1 to 2
business days.

## International shipping

We ship to Canada and the UK for a flat $18. Delivery takes 7 to 14 business
days. Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the customer.

## Tracking

You get a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships. If tracking
shows no movement for 10 days, contact us and we will reship or refund.
