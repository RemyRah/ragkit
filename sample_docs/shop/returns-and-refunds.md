# Returns & Refund Policy

## Satisfaction guarantee

If you don't love a coffee, tell us within 30 days of delivery and we will
send a replacement of a different coffee or issue a full refund, even if the
bag is opened. We just ask that you tell us what you didn't like.

## How to request a refund

Email hello@emberandoak.example with your order number. You do not need to
mail the coffee back. Refunds are issued to the original payment method and
appear on your statement within 5 to 10 business days.

## Equipment returns

Unused brewing equipment (grinders, kettles, drippers) can be returned within
30 days in its original packaging for a refund of the purchase price. Return
shipping for equipment is paid by the customer unless the item arrived
damaged or defective.

## Damaged or lost orders

If an order arrives damaged, send a photo within 7 days and we will reship it
free. If tracking shows no movement for 10 days, we will reship or refund.

## Subscriptions

Subscription charges can be refunded if you contact us before the order
ships. After it ships, the satisfaction guarantee above applies.
