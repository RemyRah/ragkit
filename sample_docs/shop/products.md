# Product Sheet

## Sunrise Blend — $16 / 12 oz

Medium roast. Brazil and Colombia. Notes of milk chocolate, toasted almond
and caramel. Our best seller for drip and cold brew.

## Ethiopia Yirgacheffe — $19 / 12 oz

Light roast, washed process. Notes of jasmine, lemon and black tea.
Best as pour-over.

## Midnight Espresso — $17 / 12 oz

Dark roast. Brazil, Sumatra and Guatemala. Notes of dark cocoa and molasses
with a heavy body. Built for espresso and moka pot.

## Decaf Swiss Water Colombia — $17 / 12 oz

Medium roast, decaffeinated with the chemical-free Swiss Water process.
Notes of brown sugar and red apple.

## Allergens and certifications

All coffees are roasted in a facility that handles only coffee. Ethiopia
Yirgacheffe and the Decaf are certified organic. Bags are compostable.
