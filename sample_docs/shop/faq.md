# Ember & Oak Coffee Roasters — FAQ

*Ember & Oak is a fictional small-batch roaster used as ragkit demo data.*

## When is my coffee roasted?

We roast every Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Orders placed before 10am Eastern
ship the same roast day, so beans reach you within a few days of roasting.

## Whole bean or ground?

Every coffee is available as whole bean or ground for drip, espresso,
French press or pour-over. Choose your grind on the product page.

## How should I store my beans?

Keep beans in the resealable valve bag they arrive in, away from light and
heat. Do not refrigerate. Coffee tastes best 4 to 21 days after roasting.

## Do you offer a subscription?

Yes. The Roaster's Choice subscription ships a new single-origin coffee every
2 or 4 weeks and saves 10% on every bag. Pause, skip or cancel any time from
your account page, with no fees.

## Do you sell gift cards?

Digital gift cards are available from $15 to $150 and never expire.

## How do I contact you?

Email hello@emberandoak.example or use the chat on our site. We answer within
one business day, Monday to Friday.
